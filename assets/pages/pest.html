Faction overview

Everything
decays.

Pestilence does not destroy its enemies all at once. It infects them—and lets time finish the work.

The Horsemen of Pestilence; a fungal hive mind that takes over living beings and uses their blood to grow. It refuses to stop until it cannot spread further.

“

What spreads cannot always be contained.

Signature strategy

Infect the weak.
Spread the rot.

Pestilence focuses on slow-burn pressure. Infect enemy units, stack debilitating effects, and allow contagion to spread across the battlefield while opponents struggle to recover.
