What is Bloodletting?

The kingdom is in play
THRONE
Four factions. One objective.

Bloodletting is an asymmetric, strategy-driven board game where players take on the roles of apocalyptic factions vying for control of a crumbling world. In the base version, you may embody the apocalypses of War, Death, Famine, and Pestilence.

Each player commands units and collects blood as currency to play action cards that shape the outcome of battles through a unique battle deck system. Victory isn't just about strength—it's about how and when you strike. Use plots, schemes, and strategy to outwit your opponents and complete your faction's unique objective to seize the Throne and take over the kingdom!

Explore hidden locations, contest enemies, and unleash chaos in this asymmetric game of war, deception, and end-times ambition.
